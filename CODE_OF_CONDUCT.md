# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss the code, not the person writing it.

## What that means here

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements in a codebase are about missing context,
  not about someone being careless.
- Critique the change, not the author. "This breaks the card cycle when the month
  has 30 days" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Keep it welcoming regardless of experience level, language, background, gender,
  identity, disability or anything else about a person. Spanish and English are
  both fine here — do not make anyone feel unwelcome for the one they chose.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexual language, or publishing
  anyone's private information.
- FinTrack deals with people's money. If an issue or PR includes someone's real
  financial data, ask for it to be removed rather than quoting it back.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commits, discussions and any other space
where someone is acting on behalf of this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **giancarlos.estevez@gmail.com**. Reports stay
private. As the maintainer, I will look into it and may edit, hide or delete
contributions, or block accounts, when that is the right call — and I hold myself
to the same standard.
